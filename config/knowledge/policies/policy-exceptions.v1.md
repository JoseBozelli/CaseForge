## When exceptions apply
An exception allows a case to deviate from a documented procedure, for example to extend a deadline. Exceptions are rare and must be justified by the case facts.

## Approval required
Every policy exception requires approval from an authorized approver before it takes effect. An agent or operator who proposes an exception cannot approve it.

## Recording exceptions
Record the exception, the approver and the reason in the case history.
